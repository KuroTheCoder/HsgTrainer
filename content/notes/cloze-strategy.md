# Cloze technique

Cloze passages test cohesion and collocation more than grammar rules. The strategy is to read **beyond the sentence** — the answer is often locked by the previous or next sentence.

## Read order

1. Skim the whole passage (60 seconds) to get the topic and tense timeline.
2. Answer in order of confidence; skip and return.
3. Re-read the full sentence (not the gap) for each remaining blank.
4. Check discourse markers: however, therefore, whereas, although — they decide contrast vs cause.

> [!note] Collocations beat rules
> "make a decision", "take responsibility", "pay attention" — most cloze blanks in HSG papers test collocation, not grammar. If two options are grammatically fine, the collocation wins.

## The tricky ones

| Marker | Job | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| however / nevertheless | contrast | opposite of previous clause |
| therefore / thus | consequence | follows from previous clause |
| whereas / while | comparison | two sides contrasted |
| moreover / furthermore | addition | continues the same direction |

```text
"The proposal was popular with the public. _____, the committee rejected it."
→ "However" (contrast). "Therefore" would mean the rejection followed from
public support — wrong direction.
```

## When you still have gaps

- [x] Read the passage aloud mentally — the right word often "sounds" right
- [ ] Eliminate by part of speech first, meaning second
- [ ] Check the key words the app shows for the question
- [ ] Look up unknown words in the dictionary tool, then re-answer

> [!quote] Exam-day rule
> Every blank you skip counts as wrong. Always submit something — a wrong guess is worth the same as an empty box, and the explanation on the results screen teaches either way.
