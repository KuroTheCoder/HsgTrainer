# Welcome to the notes vault

These are the maintainer's study guides, curated from the Obsidian vault and rendered right here in the app. Read them, drill them, and revisit the sections they point to.

- Start with [[word-formation]] — the biggest scoring section in most tỉnh/thành phố papers.
- Then [[cloze-strategy]] for the cloze passages.
- New guides appear here as they get curated; the app never shows graph view, canvas, or plugins — open the vault in Obsidian for those.

## What you can do here

1. Browse the sidebar or search by title, tag, or content.
2. Click a wikilink to jump between notes.
3. `#tags` in the text link nowhere but show the topic at a glance.

> [!tip] A habit that works
> Read a guide, then run a practice session on that section with the tag filter. Wrong answers land in My mistakes; the ledger keeps only your latest attempt per question.
